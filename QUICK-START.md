# Quick Start - 3 Ways to Deploy Your Tool

## ✅ Option 1: GitHub Pages (Best for Sharing)

**What you need:**
- Access to https://github.com/brendadlawrenc3-cloud/Alignment-Tool-for-Executives
- Admin/Settings permission on that repository

**Steps:**
1. Go to: https://github.com/brendadlawrenc3-cloud/Alignment-Tool-for-Executives/settings/pages
2. Under "Branch", select: `claude/executive-alignment-scorecard-011CUdwyVvyuJ2eohuSjqCr4`
3. Click "Save"
4. Wait 3 minutes
5. Your site will be live!

**Result:** https://brendadlawrenc3-cloud.github.io/Alignment-Tool-for-Executives/

---

## ✅ Option 2: Use Your Local Computer (Easiest!)

**What you need:**
- The files already on your computer (they're at `/home/user/Alignment-Tool-for-Executives/`)

**Steps:**

### If you're on Mac/Linux:
```bash
cd /home/user/Alignment-Tool-for-Executives
python3 -m http.server 8080
```
Then open: http://localhost:8080/index.html

### If you're on Windows:
1. Right-click `index.html`
2. Choose "Open with" → Your browser (Chrome, Firefox, etc.)

**Result:** Tool works on your computer instantly!

---

## ✅ Option 3: Netlify Drop (Super Easy, 30 seconds!)

**What you need:**
- A free Netlify account (netlify.com)
- The files on your computer

**Steps:**

1. **Download your files as a ZIP:**
   - All files are in: `/home/user/Alignment-Tool-for-Executives/`
   - Or use the archive I created: `/home/user/alignment-tool.tar.gz`

2. **Go to Netlify Drop:**
   - Visit: https://app.netlify.com/drop
   - Sign up for free (or login)

3. **Drag and drop:**
   - Drag your entire project folder to the Netlify Drop zone
   - That's it!

4. **Get your URL:**
   - Netlify instantly gives you a live URL like: `https://random-name-12345.netlify.app`
   - You can customize this URL in settings

**Result:** Live site in 30 seconds!

---

## 🎯 Which Option Should You Choose?

### Choose **Option 1 (GitHub Pages)** if:
- ✅ You have access to the GitHub repository
- ✅ You want a permanent, professional URL
- ✅ You want automatic updates when you change code

### Choose **Option 2 (Local)** if:
- ✅ You just want to test/preview the tool
- ✅ You're not ready to share publicly
- ✅ You want to see it RIGHT NOW

### Choose **Option 3 (Netlify)** if:
- ✅ You don't have GitHub access or it's not working
- ✅ You want instant deployment (30 seconds!)
- ✅ You want a simple drag-and-drop solution
