# Executive Alignment Scorecard

**Is your leadership team fully aligned? Find out in minutes.**

## Overview

The Executive Alignment Scorecard is a web-based assessment tool designed to help leadership teams measure and improve their alignment across critical dimensions. Most executive teams score just 63% on alignment - discover where your team excels and where gaps are holding you back.

## Features

- **5 Core Dimensions**: Measure alignment across Strategy, Role Clarity, Decision-Making, Culture, and Execution
- **Comprehensive Assessment**: 30 carefully crafted questions (6 per dimension)
- **Instant Scoring**: Get immediate results with color-coded performance indicators
- **Actionable Insights**: Receive specific recommendations for each dimension
- **Benchmarking**: Compare your team's performance against top-performing executive teams
- **PDF Reports**: Generate and download detailed assessment reports
- **Mobile Responsive**: Complete assessments on any device

## Assessment Dimensions

1. **Strategic Alignment** - Shared vision and priorities
2. **Role Clarity** - Clear responsibilities and decision rights
3. **Decision-Making & Accountability** - Efficient decisions and follow-through
4. **Culture & Values** - Consistent behaviors and norms
5. **Execution & Metrics** - Aligned success measures

## How to Use

1. Open `index.html` in a web browser
2. Read the introduction and click "Start Assessment"
3. Answer all 30 questions honestly (1-5 scale)
4. Review your team's alignment score and insights
5. Download the PDF report for team discussion
6. Implement recommended action steps

## Scoring System

- **1-5 Scale**: Strongly Disagree (1) to Strongly Agree (5)
- **Dimension Scores**: Average of questions in each dimension
- **Overall Score**: Average of all 5 dimension scores
- **Color Coding**:
  - 🔴 Red (1.0-2.9): Needs Immediate Attention
  - 🟡 Yellow (3.0-3.9): Moderate - Room for Improvement
  - 🟢 Green (4.0-5.0): Strong Alignment

## Benchmarking

- **Top Performers**: 4.2+ overall score
- **Average Teams**: 3.1-3.2 overall score
- **Struggling Teams**: Below 3.0 overall score
